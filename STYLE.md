## Empire in Minutes History (empire) — English history (mythology counts), must not drift from history
- 9:16 720x1280, 30 fps, ~30-45 s.
- Layout: small lip-synced cartoon narrator at the bottom; big top "screen" where the story plays; top shows only a title card (no counters).
- Top screen mixes real public-domain historical images (Art Institute of Chicago / Wikimedia, high-res) with slow Ken Burns (subpixel, no shaking) and varied reveals (iris, wipe, framed drop-in) + flat 2D cartoon scenes (thick dark outlines, soft grain, navy/indigo room, teal monitor style).
- Script: second-person "you" scenario. Hook states era/place + the "you" situation in first 3 s -> one rule/stake -> time limit -> dangers, each with a one-line real historical reason -> loss condition -> end card exactly: "Would YOU survive? Type SURVIVE or DEAD."
- Captions under the screen: bold rounded sans (Montserrat/Poppins ExtraBold style), white with soft shadow, 3-6 words, key words yellow, word-synced to voice.
- Voice: male deep calm dramatic narrator, ~3 words/s, no long pauses. Music: soft sweet music-box bed ~17-20 dB under voice, never bass-heavy. No transition SFX. Smooth gradual scene changes every ~2-4 s.
