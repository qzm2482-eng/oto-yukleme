## Packaging rules (vidIQ research 21 Nov 2026, owner asked once)
- Title scoring (vidIQ, Shorts): naming the mission/thing beats a vague hook — "Lightning Hit Apollo 12 Twice During Launch" 89 vs "Their Rocket Got Hit by Lightning. Twice." 87. Rule: specific name + dramatic verb + number, under ~50 chars, no clickbait lies.
- Thumbnail/title changes that doubled views in space/NASA niche: 2-3 huge words, one keyword in yellow, often a question or "NASA SAW THIS" style curiosity line, one big clear subject, a bold arrow/circle pointing at it, dark/high-contrast background; dollar/number hooks ("$17 BILLION SECRET").
- Our cover = real photo zoomed on the key detail, light blur only, hot glow/sparks where relevant, red circle + arrow, small inset of the whole object for context, red label (place · year), title white + yellow keyword (question mark when it fits).
